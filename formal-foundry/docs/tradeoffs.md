# Formal Foundry Trade-offs

## Accessibility versus expressive power
A more expressive specification language can become harder to learn. The project should favor clarity over maximal theoretical reach in early stages.

## Bounded checking versus full proof systems
Bounded exploration is more practical to build and easier to explain, but provides weaker guarantees than full proof approaches.

## Human-readable failures
Readable counterexamples are essential for adoption, even if they require more work than raw solver output.
