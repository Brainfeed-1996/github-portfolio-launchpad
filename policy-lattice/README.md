# Policy Lattice

Policy evaluation and decision-explanation platform for rules, constraints, layered controls, and governance automation.

## Why this project matters

Policy systems are everywhere, but often poorly modeled. Policy Lattice shows how to design a dedicated engine for decisions that must be explainable, composable, and enforceable across products and platforms.

## Core capabilities

- policy model and schema
- layered rule evaluation
- decision explanation traces
- simulation and dry-run workflows
- policy versioning and rollout controls

## Suggested stack

- Rust, Go, or TypeScript depending on runtime goals
- optional web console for policy inspection

## High-ROI signal

Strong for platform, compliance automation, security policy, governance, and internal tooling roles.
